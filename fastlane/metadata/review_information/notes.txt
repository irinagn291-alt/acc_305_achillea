Thanks for reviewing Achillea. We retested the build under review on a clean device. From the splash through onboarding, the list, History, Settings and How Draw works, we could not reproduce a crash, a dead end, an empty screen or a non-functioning feature.

Three behaviours are deliberate.

1. "Save names" stays disabled until "First name" and "Second name" both have text. "Add" stays disabled until "Add a name" has text. Type the missing name and the button enables.
2. After a pick, names freeze and only "New list" continues. Status is "Saved"; the line is "That pick is in History. Start a new list for another." "New list" keeps the names and clears the lock and pick.
3. History can open on "No picks yet." / "Draw a name, then read it here." Settings can open on "Nothing stored yet." / "Add two names on the list, then come back." Use "Back to the list" or Close, save names, Draw once, then reopen.

Full review path. No account, no login:

- Page 1: "One honest pick from names you type.", "Skip", "Continue". Page 2: "Lock the list. Then Draw." Page 3: "Filed picks stay on this device." Page 4: "Even chance treats two names the same.", "Begin" (no "Skip"). "Skip" or "Begin" opens the list titled "Achillea".
